Earning so much more, owning the same

Movement 1 - The Felt Problem

You are Muhammad, years into the journey now, and by one measure you've soared: you earn far more than in the lean early days - back then, this income would have felt like a fortune, a dream. You should be wealthy by now.

So one quiet evening you actually add it up - what you OWN - and the number stops you cold. A little in the bank. No real assets. No growing security. Almost exactly where you were years ago, when you earned a fraction of this. Where did it all GO? Not on anything wild - on a slightly bigger place, more family you're glad to help, better everyday things, a dozen sensible upgrades that each felt completely reasonable at the time. And yet, added together, they ate every extra rand you ever earned.

The truth lands quietly and it chills you: every time the money grew, your life grew to swallow it. You've been running faster and faster just to stand in the same place. And if nothing changes, you can see it plainly - ten more years could pass exactly like the last ten, earning more and more, owning no more at all.

I earn so much more than I used to - so why do I own barely more than I did? Where does it all keep going, and how do I make it finally STAY?

Movement 2 - Your Attempt

Your hope has always been the simplest one: earn a bit more, and then I'll get ahead. But look at the evidence of your own life - you DID earn more, again and again, and it changed nothing. Each raise just got swallowed like all the others. And "I'll save whatever's left over" has failed every month, because there's never anything left - spending quietly stretches to fill whatever comes in.

Before you read on: if a bigger income gets absorbed every time, and saving the leftovers never works, what would have to change about the ORDER or the SETUP - not the amount - for money to actually stay?

A bigger income won't fix it - every raise so far just got swallowed the same way. The problem isn't how much comes in; it's that nothing is set up to make any of it STAY.
I save 'whatever's left' - and there's never anything left, because spending quietly grows to fill whatever I earn. What if I kept my piece FIRST, and lived on the rest?

Movement 3 - The Worked Solution

Here is the heart of it: money flows THROUGH you unless you design it to STAY. By default, whatever comes in finds a way out - so getting ahead was never going to come from earning more; it comes from building something that catches and keeps the gap. That design has three parts, decided once:

  • Money flows THROUGH you unless you design it to STAY: your income rose for years, but what you OWN barely moved - because outflow silently rises to meet inflow. It's not recklessness; it's that nothing is built to catch and keep the gap.
  • Name a target (so 'enough' exists): decide a real number for the security and life you want, reasoned backwards. Without a destination, 'save more' is a wish; with one, every rand kept has a job.
  • Reverse the order - keep FIRST, live on the rest: you'll always adapt to whatever's left after spending - so make 'what's left' smaller on purpose. Set aside your keep-amount the day income lands, then live on the remainder. (Pay yourself first.)
  • Pre-commit your raises - the master move: before your next increase even arrives, decide most of it goes to keeping, not lifestyle. Here's the secret: banking money you never got used to is painless - while clawing money back from a lifestyle you've grown into is agony. Shape future money; don't chase spent money.
  • Decide once, automate, done: this isn't daily willpower. Set the target, set the automatic transfer, assign the next raise - then it runs itself while you live your life.

Give the third one its full weight, because it is the most powerful and the least painful move in all of money: pre-commit your raises before they arrive. Think of the asymmetry. To save more from your life as it is now, you'd have to claw money back from a lifestyle you've already grown into - and that hurts, every single day. But money from a raise you haven't received yet? You never got used to it, so setting it aside costs you nothing - you simply never miss it. The same rand is agony to reclaim and effortless to never-spend. That gap is where wealth is quietly built.

And let this be clear: none of it is about deprivation or abandoning the people you love. It is about keeping first, by design, then living - generously - on what remains. You'd be amazed how full a life "the rest" still buys.

Movement 3.5 - The Guided Step

So Muhammad stops hoping and starts designing - and the numbers show him exactly what happened, and what to do.

See where the money went - and how to make it stay

What happened: in the lean days he earned about R6,000 a month and kept roughly R0. Today he earns about R20,000 - and STILL keeps roughly R0. So the entire R14,000 raise, built over years, went straight into lifestyle. Every cent of "earning more" became "spending more."

The redesign - keep first, on purpose: he names a target (a full cushion, then real assets), and sets up R4,000 to move out automatically the day his income lands - into savings, before he can touch it. He lives on the remaining R16,000.

The thing that kills the fear: R16,000 is not poverty - it's still nearly THREE TIMES the R6,000 he once built a whole life on. He isn't going without; he's living well AND finally keeping the gap. Nothing about his real comfort collapses.

The master move: when his next raise comes, half of it is already spoken for - it goes to keeping before it ever reaches his hand. He never feels its loss, because he never felt its arrival.

The result: in a few short years he owns what a whole decade of "earning more" never gave him - a cushion, then assets, then real security. The money finally stays.

Movement 4 - In Your Own World

You were never going to win this by earning more - the leak was in the design, not the income. Fix the design once, and ordinary income quietly becomes real wealth. Whatever flows to you - a wage, a grant, a business surplus:

  1. Name a target. Decide a real number for the security you want (your emergency cushion first, then real assets), so "enough" exists and every rand kept has a job - without a destination, saving is just a wish.
  2. Keep first, live on the rest. Set your keep-amount aside the day money lands - made automatic, before you can spend a cent. You will adapt to what is left, exactly as you always have.
  3. Pre-commit your next raise. Decide NOW that most of your next increase goes to keeping - before it arrives and before you grow used to it. This is the painless way the gap becomes wealth.

The difference between a life of money flowing through your hands and a life of money staying in them is not how much you earn - it's a design you can decide today. The single riskiest choice you can make is to change nothing and let another decade vanish the same way. Keep first, give every raise a job before it arrives, and let an ordinary income build you an extraordinary security.
