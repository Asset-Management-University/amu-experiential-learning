Movement 1 - The Felt Problem

You are Onkgopotse, and you have just spent the week learning the basics of how tax works - and tonight, at the kitchen table, a thought arrives that turns you cold. You are thinking about your Aunty Tlhompho.

Aunty Tlhompho is the most honest, hard-working person you know. She has worked at the same hospital for thirty years. She has been on the staff medical aid for as long as you have been alive, covering herself and her two children. Every month, faithfully, a piece of her salary has gone into the work retirement fund. She has never cheated anyone of a single cent in her life.

And that is exactly when the cold feeling comes. Because in everything you have just learned, there were these doors built into the tax law - rebates, deductions - that lower what you owe, precisely to reward people for things like being on a medical aid and saving for retirement. The things Aunty Tlhompho has done, dutifully, for THREE DECADES. And you realise, with a sinking heart, that no one ever told her. She just paid the first tax number they showed her, every year, and got on with her life. You start to wonder how much was quietly lost.

My aunt has been on a medical aid and saving for retirement her whole working life - thirty years - and she is the most honest person I know. Does that mean every single year she just handed over thousands of rand that was legally HERS to keep, simply because no one ever told her she could claim it back? Was it just... gone?

"Tax is far too complicated - I'll never understand it, so why try?" But you do not have to become an expert; you only need to know the doors exist, keep your records, and get help to file. Each of these thoughts feels safe, and each one is exactly what cost Aunty Tlhompho.

Movement 3 - The Worked Solution

Nobody robbed Aunty Tlhompho. The money was hers all along - she simply never knew there was a door, or a key. Five moves:

  • There are two tax numbers, not one: The tax figure you first see - the one taken off your pay, or the one on an automatic assessment - is a STARTING point, worked out from what the taxman already knows. Your true, final tax is only settled after you have claimed every legal rebate and deduction you are entitled to. Most people never realise there is a second, lower number with their name on it.
  • The unaware overpay - for years: Here is the quiet injustice: if you do not know your right to claim, you will simply overpay, year after year, and never feel it happen. The taxman will not chase you to give your money back - the responsibility to claim it is yours alone. An honest, hard-working person can hand over thousands of rand a year that was always legally theirs, purely because no one ever told them about the doors.
  • The menu of legal doors - your right, not tricks: The law builds in specific ways to lower your tax, on purpose, to reward good things. They are your RIGHTS, not dodges: medical-aid tax credits (a fixed amount off your tax for being on a scheme); retirement-savings deductions (the taxman refunds tax on what you put away for retirement); the tax-free savings account (all its growth untaxed); and a tax-free slice of interest each year. If you run a business, the expenses you must spend to earn your income come off before tax too. Using these is exactly what they are FOR.
  • The one key: awareness plus good records: Every one of those doors needs the same key, and it is wonderfully simple: AWARENESS - knowing the doors exist - plus GOOD RECORD-KEEPING - having the certificate or receipt to PROVE each claim. You can only claim what you know about and can prove. A folder (on paper or your phone) holding your medical certificate, your retirement certificate, and your receipts is, quite literally, the thing that keeps your money yours.
  • Be the one who knows: So this is the change you can be: the person in your family who finally KNOWS. Be aware the doors exist, keep your records, file a correct return that claims what you are owed, get help if you are unsure - and then teach the next person. These rules are not taught in school, which quietly keeps them the advantage of those in the know. Learning them is your right - and bringing them home to the people you love can keep real money in their pockets for the rest of their lives. (Strictly legal - this is claiming what the law gives you, never hiding income; rules change, so check the latest and get proper help.)

The whole thing lives in one line: the tax you first calculate is not always the tax you must pay - the law gives you legal doors to lower it, and the key that unlocks them all is awareness plus good record-keeping. What stung about Aunty Tlhompho was never that she did anything wrong - she did everything right, for thirty honest years. It was that the rules of her own money were quietly kept from her, and a silence no one broke cost her, year after year, what was always hers. But silence is the only thing standing between any of us and that money - and you have just broken it. Now you know the doors are real, that claiming them is your right and not a trick, and that a simple folder of records is the key. Hold that knowledge, keep your records, and one day carry it home to the people you love.

Movement 3.5 - The Guided Step

Before you take this home, see exactly what the silence cost - and what breaking it gives back - in plain Rand.

See one year of Aunty Tlhompho's overpaid tax, and the refund that fixes it

Take a single year. Aunty Tlhompho is on a medical aid covering herself and her two children, and she puts R30,000 a year into her retirement fund. She never claimed either. Here is what the law would have given back:

Medical-aid tax credits (a fixed amount off her tax for being on a scheme): about R364 for herself + R364 for the first child + R246 for the second child = R974 a month, which is R11,688 for the year - taken straight off her tax bill.

Retirement-savings deduction: her R30,000 of retirement contributions is deductible, so the taxman refunds the tax on it. At an example tax rate of 31%, that is R30,000 x 31% = R9,300 back.

Add them: R11,688 + R9,300 = nearly R21,000 she overpaid in just ONE year - money that was always legally hers. Across the years she never knew, it quietly grew into well over a quarter of a million rand - an interest-free loan to the state, given out of pure not-knowing.

Then the fix. This year, you help her. Together you gather two pieces of paper - her medical-aid tax certificate and her retirement certificate - and you file a correct return that claims them. A few weeks later, a refund lands in her account. Same aunt, same honest year - the only thing that changed was that someone finally knew about the doors, and kept the records to open them. (Illustrative figures - everyone's tax differs, and rules change; the shape of it is true for millions.)

Movement 4 - In Your Own World

You may not file a tax return yet - but you can become the person in your family who finally knows, starting now:

  1. Start the folder today, even empty. Make a simple home - a cardboard folder, or a folder on your phone - for the documents that prove your claims: a medical-aid certificate, a retirement certificate, interest statements, donation slips. You can only ever claim what you can prove, so the folder IS the key. Begin it before you have a single thing to put in it.
  2. Name the doors that fit your family. Look at your household and ask which legal doors likely apply: is someone on a medical aid? Is anyone saving into a retirement fund or a tax-free account? Earning interest? Each 'yes' is money that may be quietly leaking, and a door someone could be walking through.
  3. Resolve to know, to keep records, and to get help. You do not need to become a tax expert - you need to know the doors are real, keep your records, and get proper help (a tax helper, or free official guidance) to file a correct return. Decide now that when you earn, you will claim what is legally yours - and that you will help the people you love do the same.
