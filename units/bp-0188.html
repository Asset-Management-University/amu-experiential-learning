'Financial freedom' feels like a fog with no finish line - how do I turn it into a real number I can actually aim at?

Movement 1 - The Felt Problem

You are Phomello. Lately you keep hearing people talk about being 'financially free' one day - not having to worry about money, work becoming something you choose rather than something you are forced into. You want that too. Badly. It is a powerful, beautiful dream.

But every time you try to actually reach for it, it dissolves into fog. A shimmering haze somewhere on the horizon. You are supposed to be running towards it - but the horizon just keeps sliding away as you go. There is no finish line. There is no map. How much money is 'enough' to be free? A hundred thousand? A million? Ten million? You genuinely have no idea.

And that is the quiet problem: because you cannot see the target, you cannot tell whether anything you do brings you closer or not. Save R200 this month - are you nearer? You cannot say. So it never feels like progress, and the whole thing feels so impossibly huge that you cannot even bring yourself to take a first step. The dream just sits there, shimmering and out of reach, and stays a daydream.

How can I ever reach a goal like 'financial freedom' when it has no finish line? It is just a fog I cannot aim at - so I never even start.

Movement 2 - Your Attempt

Reaching into the fog, three answers tempt you - and each one keeps you stuck. "I'll just aim to be rich, to have a lot." But 'a lot' has no finish line either - there is always more, so you can never arrive, and you drift forever without ever feeling free. "I'll know freedom when I get there - I don't need a number." But you will not: with no number, you quietly move the goalposts every time you get close, and 'free' stays one step ahead of you for life. "Real freedom needs millions, so there's honestly no point me starting now." But that assumes it is all-or-nothing - and it is not; the whole thing scales, so you can start by buying your freedom one small piece at a time. The trouble with all three is the same: no finish line.

So before you read on: what would it actually take to turn this fog into something you could aim at - a real target, with a number on it, that you could measure yourself against?

Stay with the frustration. You want 'freedom', but what IS it, exactly? How would you ever know you had arrived? Trying to reach a goal with no finish line is like running at the horizon - it keeps moving away. What if the first job is not to run faster, but to draw the finish line?
What if 'freedom' was not a feeling at all, but a calculation? Picture it like this: you are free when your money earns enough, on its own, to cover what your life costs - so working becomes a choice, not a must. If that is the target, then the question becomes simple: how big must my pot of money be for that to happen?
There is a well-tested rule of thumb: your FREEDOM NUMBER is roughly your YEARLY costs times 25 (because drawing about 4% of an invested pot each year can cover your costs and keep going). It scales, so you can start tiny: a R100/month data bill is R1,200 a year, and R1,200 x 25 = R30,000 - reach that, invested, and your phone pays for itself. The fog just became a number.

Movement 3 - The Worked Solution

You could not aim because there was no target. So let us build one. Five moves:

  • See the trap of the fog: A goal like 'being rich' or 'being free one day' has no finish line. You can run toward that horizon your whole life and never arrive, because there is no point you can ever reach and say 'done'. That is exactly why it feels paralysing - you cannot aim at a fog, so you never take the first step. The problem was never you; it was a goal with no edges.
  • Define freedom as a state, not a dream: Real financial freedom is not about being 'rich'. It has a clear, plain definition: it is when your invested money earns enough on its own to cover your living costs - so work becomes a CHOICE, not a necessity. That is the whole prize: not a pile of cash, but your own time back. And because it is a definite state, it can be turned into a definite number.
  • Turn the state into a number - costs x 25: Here is the rule that does it (a real, well-tested rule of thumb, not a promise): your Freedom Number is about your YEARLY costs times 25. Why 25? Because if your money is invested and growing, drawing roughly 4% of it a year tends to cover your costs while the pot keeps refilling itself - and 4% is one twenty-fifth. So 25 years of costs, invested, is the rough size of pot that can pay for your life from then on.
  • Start with a finish line you can actually cross: A full Freedom Number can feel as huge and far-off as 'rich'. So do not start there - start with ONE bill. Your data costs R100 a month, which is R1,200 a year. By the same rule, R1,200 x 25 = R30,000. Reach R30,000 invested, and at about 4% a year it throws off the R1,200 to cover your data - forever, without a shift or a parent. You are 'data-independent'. Small, real, and reachable far sooner than millions.
  • Name your number and take aim: This is your FREEDOM NUMBER, and the fog is gone - it is a concrete figure on a page now. You can aim at it, measure how close you are getting, and start filling it today, one rand at a time. And the big dream you started with? It is not a different thing - it is just this exact same maths, scaled up to cover your whole life instead of one bill.

The whole shift lives in one line: freedom is not a fog called 'rich' - it is a number, your yearly costs times 25, and a number is something you can actually aim at. The reason it always felt impossible was never that you were not trying hard enough; it was that you were running at a horizon with no finish line. Draw the line - put a real figure on it - and everything changes: now you can see it, measure your steps toward it, and start with a small, reachable piece (your data covered, your transport covered) long before the whole thing. The dream did not get smaller. It just turned from a fog into a plan with a finish line you can cross.

Movement 3.5 - The Guided Step

Before you take this into your own life, watch someone turn one real bill into a finish line they can actually reach.

See how Keorapetse works out their 'transport-independent' number

Keorapetse is tired of asking a parent for taxi money, or working an extra shift just to get to college. They want the quiet dignity of their transport simply being taken care of. So instead of dreaming about being 'rich', they work out one small, exact finish line.

Step 1 - the monthly cost. Their transport comes to R250 a month.

Step 2 - the yearly cost. R250 x 12 = R3,000 a year.

Step 3 - the x 25 rule. R3,000 x 25 = R75,000.

That R75,000 is Keorapetse's 'transport-independent' number. If they had R75,000 invested and growing, drawing about 4% of it a year would give them roughly the R3,000 they need for transport - so their MONEY pays for their travel, year after year, instead of their time. R75,000 is a serious goal, but it is a real, countable one with a finish line - nothing like the fog of 'be rich'. And the day they cross it, one whole part of their life is quietly, permanently handled. (The same maths covers data at R30,000, or a whole life when you scale it up to your full yearly costs.)

Movement 4 - In Your Own World

Now put a real number on your own freedom - start small, where you can actually see the finish line:

  1. Pick one bill and make it your first finish line. Choose a monthly cost you would love your money to cover for you - data, transport, a subscription. Multiply it by 12 for the year, then by 25. That figure is your first 'X-independent' number: reach it invested, and that bill is covered for life at about 4% a year. It is small enough to actually aim at.
  2. Sketch your rough full number. Make a rough guess of what your whole life costs in a year, and multiply by 25. Do not worry that it is big or imprecise - the point is that the fog now has a figure. You can refine it for years; today you just need to see it.
  3. Connect it to your saving. If you are already paying yourself a slice first each month, see that money as the first deposits filling your number - every rand now has a destination you can point at. If you are not saving yet, this number is the reason to start: a real finish line to aim that first slice at.

For as long as 'freedom' stayed a fog, it stayed impossible - not because you could not reach it, but because no one can reach a target with no finish line. You have just drawn the line. It is a number now: small enough to start on this week, real enough to measure, and yours to fill one rand at a time. The big dream is still the big dream - you have simply turned it into a plan you can actually begin. That is you, taking the fog of 'someday' and making it a finish line you can run at, starting today.
