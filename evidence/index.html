The Evidence of Need Register

A bridge to the real world starts with real problems.

We teach what the world needs. This register is our proof.

Every Business Problem you will meet in your learning is grounded in evidence that the problem matters. The evidence for each one is listed here, resting on one of two pillars:

  • Research: a need identified in documented, verifiable research — every citation verified live before it entered the register.
  • Lived experience: a problem identified by people working in the field. (This class of evidence is defined; none have yet been formally registered.)

An accreditation criterion is recorded where one applies — it explains why a unit sits in a qualification — but it is not by itself evidence that the world needs the capability. Honesty requires we also note when a unit's reason is internal to the curriculum, supporting another skill. This register is a living document, governed by our Evidence of Need Standard (QMS-020-08 section G). The raw data is published openly at evidence-register.json.

AMU's standard is that every learning unit is grounded in a demonstrated, real-world need.

Of our 523 published units, 186 are grounded today by documented research or lived experience.

The remaining 337 units satisfy formal accreditation criteria — a necessary starting point, but not sufficient evidence of need by our own standard. These units are now being systematically grounded with the same real-world evidence.

This register is the live record of that work.
