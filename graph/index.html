The Map of Business Problems

This map shows how the Business Problems in our library connect. It is the architecture of capability.

Each point is a Business Problem you can solve. A solid line shows that one problem builds toward another. A dashed line shows that two problems are related.

You can pan, zoom, and select any point to open that problem and begin. The data for this map is published openly at public-graph.json.
