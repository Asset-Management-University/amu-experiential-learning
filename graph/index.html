The Map of Business Problems

This map shows how the Business Problems in our library connect. It is the architecture of capability.

Each circle is a Business Problem you can solve. The squares belong to the framework you choose below. A light line shows which framework element a Business Problem develops. The map shows only the elements developed by our published Business Problems; each framework's full record lives on its own pages. A solid navy line shows that one Business Problem builds toward another; a dashed line shows two are related.

Business Problem Technique GFMAM artefact builds toward related develops

You can pan, zoom, and select any circle to open that Business Problem and begin. Selecting a square opens the framework element's journey page. Rest on a line to read why two Business Problems connect. The data for this map is published openly: public-graph.json, lens-gfmam.json, lens-qcto-pap.json, lens-qcto-pam.json.
