The Architect's Journey

A curriculum for young people building a life of purpose and financial capability.
The Path Two streams: Financial Freedom Builder · Business Builder

At the start of your life, the path isn't always clear. You might feel the pull to build something that matters, but the questions about money, work, and the future can feel heavy.

This journey is a place to explore those questions. It is not a lecture, but a series of simulated experiences that let you learn from your own choices in a safe environment. It is built on a simple belief: you have what it takes to build a life of purpose. This is where you develop the capability to do it.

The Goal

To guide a young person from the ache of income-that-stops to the capability to build a life of financial resilience. The journey begins by helping you see the direct link between your choices and your future, one decision at a time.

The Ikigai LifeCraft Simulation

This is where the journey begins. The LifeCraft simulation is a consequence engine. By making choices in a simulated life, you experience firsthand how decisions shape your finances, your work, and your well-being. It is a safe place to learn how the world works before you apply those lessons to your own life.
