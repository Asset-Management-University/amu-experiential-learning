Physical Asset and Facilities Management IV

KM-02 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

This is the course where the asset stops being a line in a register and becomes a physical object with a life. You will appraise performance across that whole life rather than this month; choose a valuation basis on purpose, so that three right answers to different questions stop being an argument settled by seniority; plan against the landscape the asset actually sits in; choose a maintenance strategy you could hand to somebody else and have it still work; and turn eleven competing proposals and money for four into a defended portfolio. Where AMU does not teach something this qualification claims - general materials science, South African urban planning - the modules say so rather than covering it over.

Appraising performance across the whole life

Where this begins
Every indicator on the board is green and the plant is quietly eating itself. Availability is up, because maintenance windows were shortened. Cost per tonne is down, because the overhaul was deferred. Output is at record. And the corrosion under the lagging, the bearing running two degrees warmer each quarter and the spares lead time that has doubled appear on no report at all - because each of them is a fact about the asset's LIFE, and every indicator on the board is a fact about this month.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can appraise an asset's performance in the context of its whole life - in money, in materials, in the physics of how it degrades - and forecast where it is going rather than report where it has been.
