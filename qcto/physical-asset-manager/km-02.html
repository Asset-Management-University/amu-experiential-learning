Occupational Certificate: Physical Asset Manager · a journey assembled from simulated Business Problems

Why this matters

This is the course where the asset stops being a line in a register and becomes a physical object with a life. You will appraise performance across that whole life rather than this month; choose a valuation basis on purpose, so that three right answers to different questions stop being an argument settled by seniority; plan against the landscape the asset actually sits in; choose a maintenance strategy you could hand to somebody else and have it still work; and turn eleven competing proposals and money for four into a defended portfolio. Where AMU does not teach something this qualification claims - general materials science, South African urban planning - the modules say so rather than covering it over.

Where this begins
Every indicator on the board is green and the plant is quietly eating itself. Availability is up, because maintenance windows were shortened. Cost per tonne is down, because the overhaul was deferred. Output is at record. And the corrosion under the lagging, the bearing running two degrees warmer each quarter and the spares lead time that has doubled appear on no report at all - because each of them is a fact about the asset's LIFE, and every indicator on the board is a fact about this month.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can appraise an asset's performance in the context of its whole life - in money, in materials, in the physics of how it degrades - and forecast where it is going rather than report where it has been.

Where this begins
Three numbers exist for the same pump station. The insurer's is R14 million. The balance sheet says R2.1 million. The engineer, asked what it would cost to do without it for a month, says something much larger than either. Nobody is wrong. Everybody is answering a different question, and the meeting is about to choose between them by seniority.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can choose a valuation basis for the question actually being asked, defend it against the alternatives, and design the reporting that carries the number honestly to the people who must act on it.

Where this begins
The pump station is correctly specified, correctly costed and correctly maintained, and in nine years it will be in the middle of a residential township that does not exist yet. Nobody made a mistake. The asset was designed against a demand forecast; the township was approved by a process the asset's owners were never part of; and the two decisions were each sound in the room where they were taken.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can evaluate an infrastructure system against the landscape it sits in - the demand it must meet, the risk it carries, and the interests that will shape the ground around it.

Where this begins
You have been asked to defend the maintenance strategy at a meeting on Thursday. You know it works. You have run it for six years and the plant is in better condition than when you arrived. And you cannot say why it works in any language the meeting speaks - because it is not really a strategy, it is six years of good judgement, and the moment you leave it goes with you.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can choose a maintenance strategy on evidence, defend it against the named alternatives, and write it down so it outlives you - with the tactics, intervals, condition techniques and improvement targets that make it real.

Where this begins
Eleven projects are on the list and there is money for four. Every one has a sponsor who believes in it, a number that supports it, and a consequence attached to not doing it. You have three weeks. And the thing you have quietly known for a month is that at least two of them are not projects at all - they are risks somebody dressed as an investment because that is the only form the organisation accepts.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can take a set of competing demands and turn it into a defended portfolio - reading the need honestly, weighing performance against cost against risk, and knowing what the organisation's appetite for risk actually permits.
