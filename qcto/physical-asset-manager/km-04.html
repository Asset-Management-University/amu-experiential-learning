Strategic Management IV

KM-04 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

This is the course about answering to somebody. You will read an excellence model for what it actually rewards rather than what it claims; analyse an investment against a rival call on the same money; plan infrastructure against a level of service you have promised to real people rather than an average across a town; and make asset strategy answer to the business in a language both sides can hold. Four of its criteria are corporate finance AMU does not teach; the course says which, so you know the edge of what you have.

Excellence models, and analysing an investment against rivals

Where this begins
Two proposals, both good, and the board will fund one. Yours is better on every measure you can produce. Theirs is better on the one measure the board has been using for eleven years, which nobody has ever examined, and which came from an excellence model somebody adopted in 2013 without asking what it optimises.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can read an excellence model for what it actually rewards, analyse an investment against a competitor's, and track the money and the environment around both.

Out of what they want, buried in what they don't

Working capital met as a problem somebody has.

I'm clearly earning - so why am I always broke?

The debtors side of it.

If my business is clearly making a profit, how can I keep running out of money - and what do I do about a good month that leaves me broke?

And the cash side. These three are SUPPORTING, not DIRECT: they teach working capital at the scale of a small business, and the criterion asks for strategic evaluation at NQF 8. That gap is Director-reserved corporate finance and is not claimed here - along with cash flow scenario evaluation and econometric modelling, which are GAP.

Infrastructure planning and the level of service you promise

Where this begins
The reservoir was sized for the town that existed when it was designed. The town is now four times larger in one direction and unchanged in the other, and the level of service the municipality promises is the same everywhere - which means it is comfortably exceeded on one side and quietly failed on the other, and the annual report averages the two.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can plan infrastructure against real demand and a stated level of service - evaluating proposals, costing alternatives over their lives, and saying honestly where the promise is not being kept.

Making asset strategy answer to the business

Where this begins
You can describe what the assets need. The board can describe what the business needs. Both descriptions are correct and neither is written in the other's language, so the annual conversation between them is a negotiation rather than a derivation - and whichever side is more confident that year wins.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can make asset strategy answer to the business - deriving it, reflecting it in objectives, comparing strategic options on their real impacts, and carrying sustainability and climate into the derivation rather than appending them.
