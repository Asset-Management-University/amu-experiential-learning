Organisational Physical Asset Management Capability III

KM-07 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

This is the course about people, and it is the one most likely to decide whether anything else you have learned actually happens. You will change how an organisation behaves - naming the barriers, reading the culture beneath them, and measuring the gap between what an organisation says it values and what it does. And you will build the capability of the people around you deliberately, so that development is something that can be accounted for rather than a training budget that was spent.

Changing how an organisation behaves

Where this begins
The new process is better and everybody agrees it is better and nobody is using it. Not opposition - there was no meeting where anybody objected. It simply lost, quietly, to the old way, because the old way is what the shift already knows at three in the morning and the new way needs somebody to look something up.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can change how an organisation actually behaves - naming the barriers, reading the culture beneath them, and testing whether what the organisation says it values is what it does.

Building the capability of the people

Where this begins
The training budget was spent. Forty-one people attended courses. Nobody can tell you what any of them can now do that they could not do before, because attendance was recorded and capability was not - and the one person who could actually have told you retired in June, taking with her the only complete picture of who could be trusted with what.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can build capability deliberately - analysing what is needed, planning the workforce, scheduling the resource, and running the improvement that keeps it growing after you stop watching.
