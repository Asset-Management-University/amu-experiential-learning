§1 Purpose of This Work Experience Module

WM-03 assesses your ability to assess team capability against PAMS requirements, develop a capability plan, manage an organisational change, or lead supplier/contractor development. Evidence must demonstrate that you linked capability development to PAMS requirements — not generic training or HR activity.

Organisational capability is a strategic AM resource. A PAMS that defines excellent processes but lacks the people to execute them will produce the same results as no PAMS at all. The Physical Asset Manager's role is to identify capability gaps against PAMS requirements, close those gaps through targeted development, and create the conditions under which the team can apply new skills effectively.

Capability linked to PAMS: Generic training (first aid, fire safety, basic computer skills) is HR activity. AM-linked capability development addresses specific PAMS requirements: "We need close-out documentation completed accurately to feed our performance measurement system. Bongani currently completes close-outs with minimal data. We need to develop this skill because without it, our KPIs are unreliable." That chain — PAMS requirement → capability gap → development action → measured improvement — is what WM-03 assesses.

§2 What Workplace Evidence Is Required

Minimum evidence:

  • One logbook entry describing a capability assessment and development plan you produced, or an organisational change you designed and led
  • A document you produced: TNA, development plan, supplier assessment, contract specification, or change design document
  • Supervisor verification confirming your lead role

Your entry must demonstrate: the PAMS requirement that drove the capability assessment, the gap identified, the development approach chosen and why, the implementation, and the measured improvement.

§3 Worked Example Logbook Entry

Date of workplace event: February–April 2026
Organisation: KD Auto Workshop, Tembisa
My role: Kagiso Dlamini — owner/manager

In February 2026, following the PAMS design work (WM-01), I identified that the PAMS cannot function without Bongani developing two specific capabilities: (1) safe operation of the new hoists (required by OHSA s8 and PAMS §8), and (2) accurate close-out documentation (required by PAMS §9 — performance measurement feeds from close-out data). Implementation and Outcome

Hoist operator course: Bongani completed the MERSETA-accredited 2-day hoist operator course on 5–6 March 2026 at Ditsela Training Centre. Certificate issued. OHSA compliance gap closed. This was mandatory before Bongani could operate the new HY-002 replacement unit.

Close-out documentation: I introduced a one-page close-out template (actual hours, actual materials, deviations, maintenance observations) and conducted weekly review sessions for 6 weeks. I measured close-out quality weekly using three indicators: (1) timeliness (within 24h), (2) completeness (all fields populated), (3) observations present (at least one observation per job). Results:

Week 6 (end of supervised period)88%82%68%
Target≥90%≥80%≥50%

By Week 6, timeliness was approaching target (88%) and completeness had exceeded target (82%). Observations presence (68%) exceeded the 50% target. Bongani's close-out quality has materially improved, enabling the PAMS performance measurement system to function with reliable input data.

What I would do differently: I introduced the close-out template after Bongani had already been doing close-outs for 8 months using an informal system. The template represented a change to an established habit — which is always harder than establishing a new habit. If I were implementing a PAMS from scratch, I would introduce the close-out template on day one, before any habits form. Changing existing behaviour costs significantly more effort than establishing correct behaviour from the start.

Capability development insight: The close-out measurement data (timeliness, completeness, observations) shows that the improvement was real and measurable — not just an impression. A capability development plan without measurement cannot demonstrate that development occurred. The three weekly indicators also allowed for targeted coaching: when timeliness was 88% but observation presence was only 68%, we focused the week 6 review session specifically on what constitutes a valuable observation.

§5 Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Generic training as AM evidence: If the training applies to anyone in any organisation regardless of PAMS context, it is not AM capability development. The training must address a specific PAMS requirement.
  • Development without measurement: Sending someone on a course and saying "their skills improved" is not evidence. Measure the specific capability before and after.
  • Development without sustainability: Skills develop faster than they are retained if not supported by daily practice. What process, tool, or system ensures the team uses the developed capability daily — not just after a training event?

§6 Connect to Your Qualification

WM-03 applies PM-03 (Develop Organisational PAM Capability) skills to a real team development challenge. The TNA methodology, development planning, and measured improvement approach are the same. What WM-03 adds is the management accountability dimension: you are not just doing the TNA — you are accountable for the outcome. If Bongani's close-out quality had not improved, the PAMS performance measurement system would not work. The capability development is not an HR exercise — it is a strategic AM intervention.
