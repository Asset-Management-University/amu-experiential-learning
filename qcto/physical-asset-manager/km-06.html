Why this matters

Two modules, and both are about systems that outlive the people who set them up. You will evaluate advanced system development, the enterprise platform and the funding of them; and build the quality management, the metrics and the certification case that keep them honest. It is the shortest course in the qualification and the one where an unexamined decision does the most durable damage.

Advanced systems, the enterprise platform, and funding them

Where this begins
The finance committee meets on the fourth Tuesday and you present to it, and you have worked out that they are not really asking what you think they are asking. They want to know whether the number will hold. Everything you bring them is about what the assets need; nothing you bring them is about how confident anybody should be in the figure, and so they discount all of it by the same unstated amount.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can hold the financial and systems side of an asset portfolio - the reporting model, the ratios, the budgets, the funding strategy, and the liaison with the people who release the money.

Quality management, the metrics, and certification

Where this begins
The organisation has a quality system, an asset management system, a safety system and a maintenance system, and each of them is certified or audited by somebody different. A single change - moving the workshop under operations - would touch all four, and there is no view in which all four appear together, so the change will be made in one of them and discovered in the other three.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can see the organisation as one system - the quality requirements, the process maps, the structure, the systems engineering behind it, and the re-engineering that changes it deliberately.
