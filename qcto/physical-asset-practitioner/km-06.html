Producing the financial report for a maintenance operation

Where this begins
The month-end maintenance figure is out by R84,000 and you have three days to explain it. You know roughly where it came from - a batch of work orders coded to the wrong cost centre - and you have no process for finding out exactly, because nobody has ever had to reconcile this report against anything.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can produce a financial report for a maintenance operation and stand behind it - extracting the data, verifying it before it goes out, reconciling it, and knowing the accounting principles underneath.

Planning inside the enterprise asset management system

Where this begins
