Why this matters

Two modules about standing behind a number. You will produce the financial report for a maintenance operation - extracting the data, verifying it before it goes out, reconciling it, and knowing the accounting principles that make it mean the same thing to finance as to you. And you will plan inside the enterprise system, including the workflow that decides who approves what and when. Four criteria on materials and distribution requirements planning are not taught here, and the modules say so.

Producing the financial report for a maintenance operation

Where this begins
The month-end maintenance figure is out by R84,000 and you have three days to explain it. You know roughly where it came from - a batch of work orders coded to the wrong cost centre - and you have no process for finding out exactly, because nobody has ever had to reconcile this report against anything.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can produce a financial report for a maintenance operation and stand behind it - extracting the data, verifying it before it goes out, reconciling it, and knowing the accounting principles underneath.

Planning inside the enterprise asset management system

Where this begins
The planner books the job, the storeman finds the parts are not there, and the job is rescheduled - and this has happened often enough that the planners have started ordering parts twice to be safe, which is why the parts are not there.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can plan inside the enterprise system - its planning functions, the logistics that feed them, and the workflow that decides who approves what and when.
