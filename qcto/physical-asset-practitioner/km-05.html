Financial Management and Business Engineering Principles I

KM-05 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

Most asset practitioners can follow about two thirds of a financial meeting. This course closes the other third. You will learn the language of a financial report, what an enterprise system is and is not, and the terms of investment management and budgeting - so that a payback of 2.4 years becomes a judgement you can make rather than a number you were handed.

The language of a financial report

Where this begins
You are in a meeting where the numbers are being discussed and you are following about two thirds of it. Not because the ideas are hard - you understand assets better than anybody in the room - but because 'accrual', 'carrying value' and 'below the line' are being used as though everybody learned them somewhere, and you did not.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can read a financial report and use its words correctly - income, expenditure, assets, liabilities - and apply financial analysis to a real set of asset figures.

What an enterprise system is, and what it is not

Where this begins
The system was implemented three years ago and everybody uses it and nobody trusts it. The stores module says there are four of the seals; the store has none; and everybody has learned to walk to the store and look, which means the system is now a large and expensive record of what people used to do before they walked to the store.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can say what an enterprise system is for, how logistics and project work move through it, and what it takes for the number on the screen to be worth trusting.

Investment management and budgeting, defined

Where this begins
You have been told the project has a payback of 2.4 years and you do not know whether that is good. Not because you cannot do arithmetic - because nobody has ever told you what this organisation considers acceptable, and you suspect nobody has decided.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can use the terms of investment management and budgeting precisely, and apply them to a real asset decision.
