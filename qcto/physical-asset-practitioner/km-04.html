Strategic Physical Asset Management II

KM-04 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

This is the course where the practitioner starts having to answer for judgement rather than for work. You will read what a costing model produced and know what it could not see; produce a cash flow projection you can defend; find out what actually binds your assets rather than remembering four of the answers; and lead - which for most people arrives suddenly, as a promotion out of the work they were good at.

Reading what a costing model produced

Where this begins
The model says renew. You have run it three times and it says renew each time. And you have a strong feeling that the model is answering a question nobody asked - because it does not know that this reservoir serves the only clinic on that side of the river, and there is no field in it where that fact would go.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can read what a costing model produced, judge it against acceptance criteria and what the service is actually for, and place it inside strategy, governance and the plan for when things stop.

Leading asset management work

Where this begins
You were promoted because you were the best planner. Nothing about being the best planner prepared you for the fact that the two people who used to be your peers now go quiet when you walk over, and that the thing you are now measured on is whether they do good work rather than whether you do.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can lead asset management work - reading which style the situation needs, building a team that performs, developing the people in it, and planning the resource across all of them.
