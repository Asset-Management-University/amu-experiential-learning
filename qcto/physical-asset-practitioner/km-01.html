Physical Asset and Facilities Management I

KM-01 · a journey assembled from felt Business Problems

You already do this work. This course gives you the words for it - what an asset is, which kinds there are, how a hierarchy holds together, and what it means to reconcile a register against the plant standing in front of you. It also asks you to compare the maintenance systems in use and judge your own workplace against them with evidence, which is harder than it sounds and more useful than anything else in the course.

What the different maintenance systems are each for

Where this begins
Two plants, same equipment, same age. One runs planned maintenance to a calendar and the other watches vibration and intervenes when the readings move. Both work. Both sets of people are certain the other is doing it wrong, and neither has ever had to say what their approach optimises and what it costs.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can compare maintenance management systems on what each is actually for, read what condition monitoring is telling you, and evaluate the practice in your own workplace against it.
