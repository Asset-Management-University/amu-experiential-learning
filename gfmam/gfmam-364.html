GFMAM Landscape Subject 6.4
Why this matters

This is the course that gives the people who know your plant best something to actually contribute. You will account for losses honestly, multiplying availability, performance and quality rather than reporting them apart so a good headline cannot hide what the plant is failing to produce; design a structured round that makes the operator your earliest and cheapest sensor rather than a signature on a sheet; and write operating procedures a person can actually follow under pressure, without needing the one experienced colleague on shift. What changes for you is that the moment somebody notices a problem early stops being luck and becomes something you designed for.

Where this begins
The operators know the plant better than anybody and are asked for almost nothing. Their round is a signature sheet. Their handover is verbal. The one time a developing problem was caught early it was because somebody mentioned it in the tea room to a fitter who happened to care, and that story is told as a piece of good luck rather than as evidence of a system that is not collecting what it has.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can set an operating strategy that treats losses honestly, design rounds that make the operator the plant's earliest and cheapest sensor, and write operating procedures somebody can actually follow.
