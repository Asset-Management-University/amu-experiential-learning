GFMAM Landscape Subject 7.2
Why this matters

This course teaches you almost nothing new by this stage - and that is by design rather than by accident. It takes the objectives you already know how to set from what a service actually requires, the instrument you already know how to build, the cost and risk data you already hold, and the operating limits you already know how to declare, and assembles them, deliberately, into a monitoring regime that changes a decision rather than a set of indicators nobody chose for a reason. What changes for you is that a set of numbers reported monthly and reviewed without ever being acted on becomes something you know how to interrogate and rebuild.

Where this begins
There are nine indicators, all of them reported, and the plant is monitored by none of them. They are reported monthly, in arrears, to a meeting that reviews them and moves on. Three have shown the same value for a year. And when the exchanger failed, the temperature that had been drifting upward for two years was not among the nine, because the nine were chosen in a workshop about what could be measured rather than about what would tell anybody something.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can set health and performance objectives derived from what the service requires, build an instrument somebody uses, hold the cost data, see risk outcomes, set operating limits that carry meaning, and report health in a form that changes a decision.
