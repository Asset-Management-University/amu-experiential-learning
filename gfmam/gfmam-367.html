Why this matters

This is the course about the obligation nobody has budgeted for yet. You will assess environmental impact honestly across the whole life, including the disposal phase most impact work leaves out; plan the end of an asset's life as a multi-generational obligation, written for people who have not started work here yet; work out what the land needs and what has to keep being maintained after production stops; and carry all of it in the strategic plan rather than in somebody's private expectation. What changes for you is that a decommissioning cost nine years away stops being nobody's problem this year.

Where this begins
The plant will be decommissioned in about nine years, and no line in any budget refers to it. The land will need rehabilitation, the equipment will need disposal, and something will have to maintain the parts of it that stay hazardous after it stops producing. All of this is known. None of it is anybody's this year, and the people who will carry it have not started work here yet.

What you will be able to do
By the end of this module you can plan the end of an asset life as an obligation that outlasts the people planning it, assess the environmental impact honestly, rehabilitate the land, maintain what remains after production ends, and carry all of it in the strategic plan rather than in somebody's expectation.

Every step above is a Business Problem a person works through, not a topic to be covered. Steps marked callback revisit capability developed earlier. This is the real AMU curriculum, shared freely in the spirit of Ubuntu — the belief that we all grow stronger when we help each other thrive.
